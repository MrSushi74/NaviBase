Achievement

The Start of NAVI's Glory

In 2010, NAVI won 12 medals (8 of them gold) and gathered a huge fan base. The success of the players was recognized by hltv.org: Edward (5th place), starix (4th place) were included in the annual rating, and markeloff was recognized as the strongest player of the season. Zeus, on the other hand, received the title of the best captain. In October 2010, the NAVI organization expanded, joining the Dota scene. Oddly enough, Goblak and Mag initially joined the squad, but their tenure was relatively short. The NAVI DotA team was fully formed in early 2011: it consisted of Dendi, XBOCT, LightTofHeaveN, ArtStyle, and Puppey.


2011 Expansion

In 2011, FIFA, StarCraft II, and League of Legends teams were formed. The new rosters did not meet expectations and were subsequently disbanded. The exception was Yozhyk, who is considered one of the strongest FIFA players in the world today and pleases fans with his victories. He has won more than 20 medals in various competitions of different ranks. The main esports event of 2011 was The International, a Dota 2 tournament with a record prize pool of $1,000,000. It is worth noting that Valve first introduced the game to a wide audience at GamesCom in Cologne.


Transformation in 2012

This year was very important for the CS team. The team continued to show top-class play, and won several major tournaments, taking second place. However, the transition to a new discipline was inevitable: at the end of 2012, the entire team began competing in Counter-Strike: Global Offensive. The Dota team continued to delight fans. In 2012, Natus Vincere was able to reach the finals of six major tournaments and win two championships! In addition, NAVI reached the finals of The International for the second time in a row, but this time they failed to defeat another Chinese team, Invictus Gaming. However, this did not affect the further results: NAVI continued to be one of the strongest teams in the professional scene. A significant moment was NAVI's performance in another MOBA game — League of Legends. The club signed a deal with a promising European team. The team competed in a number of championships but by the end of the year, the team ceased to exist.


2013 World of Tanks Era

In June 2013, the NAVI World of Tanks roster was composed of The RED Rush players. The tankmen claimed their first trophy in September, winning the championship in Seattle. Over the next four years, the Natus Vincere tank squad won over 20 international awards and $350,000 in China, Poland, the United States, and other countries. Unfortunately, in June 2017, Natus Vincere disbanded the roster due to the rapid decline of the esports component in the game.


Triumphing Difficulties in 2015

2015 was a very successful year for Natus Vincere: the Heroes of the Storm, Hearthstone, and League of Legends rosters started working again. In Blizzard's games, NAVI achieved excellent results: the HotS division reached the semifinals of BlizzCon, and Swedish Hearthstone player Ostkaka became the world champion, winning $100,000. Despite the success, a year later both teams were disbanded. In League of Legends, things were much worse: the European roster lasted only a short time, and the performance of the CIS compositions declined from season to season. At the end of the summer of 2017, the organization decided to focus on other popular games. In total, more than 40 trophies were won in 2015, and the prize pool exceeded $1 million!


Rise of NAVI 2016

2016 began with the CS:GO team's victories at DreamHack ZOWIE Leipzig 2016 and Counter Pit Season 2, but then experienced a long decline. In an attempt to regain the form of the game, it was decided to replace the legendary Zeus with the young and talented Alexander "s1mple" Kostylev. Alexander quickly adapted to the new team, and by the end of the year NAVI had become champions of ESL One: New York. In the US, s1mple won the first MVP award in his career! The Dota 2 composition has undergone significant changes after an unsuccessful performance at The International 2016 - there will be a team built around Dendi and GeneRaL. At the end of December, fans were presented with a new roster: European players Pajkatt, rmN- and Biver joined the "old players". In total for 2016, NAVI players won more than 35 championships, earning about $ 1,400,000.


More Challenges in 2017

2017 was not the best year for the organization: the teams in Counter-Strike: Global Offensive and Dota 2 could not return to their previous form, so the next changes were made to their roster. At the end of July, Crystallize and RodjER joined the Dota 2 team, replacing the European players, and the captain's duties were taken over by SoNNeikO. The updated line-up played with renewed vigor: they reached the semifinals of DreamLeague Season 8, won bronze at MDL Macau, and brilliantly defeated Virtus.pro during the finals of Adrenaline Cyber ​​​​League. After a series of failures that followed the CS:GO team, GuardiaN decided to leave the ranks of the organization and join FaZe Clan. At that time, there was no leader in the team, so the return of Danylo "Zeus" Teslenko was greeted with enthusiasm. Despite this, the team continued to struggle, and by the end of the year, there was a change of players: the seized became inactive, and his place was taken by the electronic one. This reshuffle had a positive effect on the squad's results, as the squad managed to qualify for several major tournaments. In 2017, Natus Vincere formed the Quake Champions and Paladins teams. Anton "COOLLERZ" Singov - winner of numerous international prizes for Quake III Arena - was the one who represented the Natus Vincere colors in the new shooter by id Software. Anton won the first gold for NAVI a few days after joining the organization, and he was able to break into the top three at the World Championship. Unfortunately, Quake Champions did not see the proper development of the pro scene: the number of championships decreased rapidly, and in May 2018 the Natus Vincere organization announced the release of the roster. As for Paladins, former WASD Sports members were the ones who represented NAVI. Currently, the NAVI.Paladins roster is one of the strongest in this discipline: the players have become world champions three times, with total earnings of $175,000. The Dota 2 team suffered many setbacks in the first half of 2018: after a series of losses, RodjER (moved to Virtus.pro) and SoNNeikO left the team. NAVI failed to recover from the loss of talented support players: the results with Lil and LeBron - newcomers to the team - were far from satisfactory, and after the defeat in the open qualifiers for The International 2018, it was decided to move the roster to inactive positions.


2018 Glory

In 2018, Natus Vincere started the Playerunknown's Battlegrounds roster - a popular Battle Royale game. The PUB lineup won the hearts of fans at the debut tournament: the LAN final of the Global Loot League Season 1 with a prize pool of $50,000 was claimed by NAVI. In Minsk, where the final qualifying round of the World Championship was held for the CIS, NAVI won again, reaffirming the status of the best team in the region. At the PUBG Global Invitational 2018, NAVI became fourth in first-person mode, earning $60,000. The CS:GO roster also continued to delight fans with victories and significantly strengthened their position on the world stage. NAVI won bronze medals at ELEAGUE Major Championships: Boston 2018, ESL Pro League Season 7 and ELEAGUE CS:GO Premier, silver at DreamHack Masters Marseille 2018 and SL i-League Season 4, and added three trophies to the collection: SL i- League Season 5, CS:GO Asia Championship and ESL One: Cologne 2018. After a series of impressive performances, Natus Vincere was placed on the second line of the world rankings, and Alexander "s1mple" Kostylev was awarded five MVP awards - he became the first player in CS:GO history to win this title without winning a tournament. At FACEIT Major: London 2018, Born to Win proved once again that they are one of the best teams in the world: NAVI stopped one step away from the trophy, losing to Astralis in the final match. On September 1, 2018, the club revealed a new Dota 2 line-up: for the first time in 8 years Dendi was not among the players. The new players were the main ones this time: Blizzy, MagicaL and Chuvash joined Crystallize, while SoNNeikO took over the captain's position. There were also changes in the coaching staff: Mag became the new mentor of the players. The most important event for the NAVI esports club in the fourth quarter of 2018 was the transition to a new owner. Oleksandr Kokhanovskyi made the decision to sell NAVI, realizing that significant investments were needed to bring the club to a new level. Then, in Q4 2018, Maksym Krippa became the majority owner of NAVI. Maksym is a Ukrainian businessman and venture capital investor with IT, Game Development, and Real Estate assets.


2019 NAVI Dynamics

2019 was a busy and fruitful year for NAVI. The esports club is represented in various disciplines, from CS:GO, Dota 2 to Battle Royale games. The CS:GO team has experienced some ups and downs. The NAVI team has been at the top of various championships, but could not boast of consistent play. For this reason, legendary players Zeus and Edward stepped down, making room for Boombl4 and GuardiaN. B1ad3, who held the position of esports director, became the new coach, and together the team aimed to conquer new heights. In 2019, thanks to investments from the new owner, the NAVI esports club launched a unique project aimed at developing young esports players: NAVI Esports Camp. Based on its results, NAVI Junior will be formed — an academy team consisting of the strongest juniors. This year was a mixed one for NAVI Dota 2. The team managed to reach The International but only finished in 13th-16th place, the team went through several roster changes and for a long time could not reach their best form. Despite this, the team ended the season on a positive note, becoming one of the participants in DreamLeague Season 13. In 2019, five new players got the yellow and black colors that represent NAVI in the new discipline Rainbow Six Siege. The debut was not the most successful, and soon after, MnM Gaming players joined and started playing for NAVI. The new roster achieved greater success, winning several regional championships, as well as the tenth season of the Pro League, taking home $100,000. The PUBG and Apex Legends divisions also showed great success, with both teams performing well in these championships and delighting the audience with great gameplay. In addition, NAVI was once again present at Quake Champions, since cYpheR returned from coaching in Apex Legends.


Rise of 2020

It was a tough year for NAVI and esports in general. There were only a few LAN events and most competitions were moved online due to the COVID-19 pandemic. 2020 started with a change in NAVI CS:GO roster. Youngster Perfecto joined the team, replacing GuardiaN. The revamped roster won a spot at BLAST Premier Spring Finals and finished second at ICE Challenge 2020 before advancing to IEM Katowice 2020. There, the team was unmatched: 10 years after their stunning victory at IEM Season IV, NAVI once again lifted the coveted trophy and took home $250,000 in prize money. NAVI Academy continued to grow and develop new players. At their peak, they were ranked 37th in the HLTV.org rankings. By the end of the year, Academy graduate B1t began to play occasionally for the main roster and eventually became a permanent member of the team. Unsatisfactory results in Dota 2 led to frequent roster changes. The turning point came in September when the entire FlyToMoon team joined the forces of Natus Vincere. Fans warmly welcomed the new players, and the club's veterans were happy to see GeneRaL and RodjER back on the roster. Things gradually improved, and in December the team took first place at OGA Dota Pit Season 4. In 2020, the club reopened its Fortnite division, inviting young players Putrick and 7tor, while also branching out into mobile esports: the PUBG Mobile division was launched in the fall. Within a few months, the two Fortnite players earned over $25,000, and the PUBG Mobile team won the EMEA League.


2021 Domination

Despite the challenges of the past year, the NAVI esports club started 2021 with big plans and clear goals. NAVI, focused on consolidating achievements and reaching new heights, NAVI began to appear not only on computer screens but also on mobile devices. Rocket League, Halo Infinite, Wild Rift, Brawl Stars, Free Fire, and VALORANT, conquering these disciplines is one of NAVI's missions. Meanwhile, the existing teams boast excellent results: for example, PUBG Mobile performed great on the international stage and regularly managed to reach the top of the championship. NAVI Counter-Strike: Global Offensive reached its highest peak in 2021. Born to Win won a number of tournaments, and the victory at PGL Major Stockholm 2021 was the highlight. In addition, three NAVI players (b1t, electronic, s1mple) entered the final Top10 ranking by HLTV.org, while s1mple topped the ranking. Also in 2021, a high-tech office with a gaming penthouse was built in Kyiv, and the club's staff increased 4-fold.


Enduring 2022

In 2022, NAVI was supposed to continue its rapid growth, but the full-scale war launched by Russia on February 24 forced the club to change its plans. The Ukrainian people did not flinch in the face of this aggression and united as never before. The NAVI esports club has been and continues to actively support Ukraine during the war. NAVI has organized many charity matches. Among them, it is worth highlighting the fundraising for the "Zemliachky" project and the BORN TO BE BRAVE tournament, organized with the United24 foundation of Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky. In collaboration with professional footballers, influencers, and the best CS:GO clubs in the world, Natus Vincere managed to raise more than $520,000. NAVI also filmed "Leaving Home" - a documentary covering the story of fans who were forced to flee the war. In addition, the club, together with Accordbank, sent 220 fully equipped cars to the front line. The war has made adjustments to the list of disciplines in which NAVI is represented. The club has disbanded the Russian PUBG Mobile and Mobile Legends rosters. We also decided to remove the League of Legends: Wild Rift team due to the low profitability of the discipline. There were also some pleasant additions to the club. In April, NAVI introduced the women's CS:GO team Javelins, which by the end of the year became one of the best in the world and qualified for the first LAN tournament in 2023. Another new roster is the women's VALORANT team NAVI Celestials, which is currently ranked third in the EMEA region. Speaking of VALORANT, another important event should be highlighted: NAVI became one of 30 partner clubs for the new Franchise League in Riot's newest discipline. Among those invited to compete is the champion roster FunPlus Phoenix led by the legendary Ukrainian captain ANGE1. Previously, the team won the VCT 2022: Stage 2 Masters LAN tournament in Copenhagen, and after joining NAVI, they immediately won the G-Loot VALORANT Cash European Championship and secured the title as one of the best in the region. No less exciting for NAVI was CS:GO. At the end of May, the team made a player change, benching Boombl4. In difficult times, sdy came to the rescue and helped NAVI win the BLAST Premier: Spring Finals 2022 tournament and take 5–8th place at the IEM Rio Major. However, the team's performance could hardly be called consistently successful. At the end of the year, the team made another overhaul, replacing sdy with npl, a promising graduate of the club's academy. It is also worth noting the transfer of headtr1ck to Ninjas in Pyjamas: he became the fourth NAVI Junior player to play at the tier-1 level. Despite the difficult season, NAVI players received several prestigious HLTV awards. In the ranking of the best players of the year B1t took 16th place, B1ad3 was the manager of the year, Perfecto was the anchor of the year, and s1mple was once again named the best player of the year, setting a new world record. This award was won along with the nomination for "Player of the Decade" from ESL, which was also won by s1mple. Dota 2, Brawl Legends, and Halo Infinite teams participated in almost all major championships in 2022. Although the teams occasionally achieved success in small tournaments, overall it was a difficult year for them.


New Record in 2023

2023 was a busy year for NAVI: we switched to the latest version of Counter-Strike, substantially updated the roster, expanded the number of European teams, and launched divisions in new disciplines. The transition from CS:GO to CS2, which marked the beginning of a new era for the discipline, was a significant moment for the club. In the summer, Perfecto, electronic, and npl left the NAVI roster, making way for a new group - Aleksib, jL, and iM. Autumn brought another significant change: the legendary s1mple went into inactivity, passing the baton to rising talent w0nderful. The new roster took 3-4th place at the prestigious BLAST Premier: World Final 2023. In CS:GO, NAVI set a number of impressive records, becoming the only team to participate in all Majors, with s1mple winning a record-breaking number of MVP medals. The team has participated in a total of 132 ESL tournaments and won 27 of them, making us the second in the world in terms of prizes won and unmatched in terms of fan support: 4 out of 5 most watched matches in this discipline featured NAVI players. Our team also had great performances in other disciplines. NAVI took part in the Brawl Stars, Clash of Clans, Halo Infinite, and VALORANT world championships. Our year ended with a strategic change: the female VALORANT rosters of Halo Infinite, Brawl Stars, and Celestials were all disbanded. At the same time, the NAVI tag returned in two disciplines, PUBG Mobile and StarCraft 2, with a revamped Ukrainian PUBG roster. Throughout 2023, NAVI took an active part in charity initiatives supporting Ukraine. Together, we raised $44,000 to rebuild a computer classroom at the Mykhailo-Kotsyubinskyi Lyceum, which was destroyed by a missile strike. In recognition of our philanthropic efforts throughout the year, NAVI received a new trophy from United24 signed by the President of Ukraine, Volodymyr Zelenskyy.


2024 Victory

In 2024, Natus Vincere (NAVI) continued to show their dominance in the esports world with outstanding achievements across multiple disciplines. The CS:GO team took top spots in several major tournaments, showing impressive performances despite roster changes. S1mple, as one of the leading players, continued to maintain his status with several MVP awards and record-setting achievements. On the other hand, the women's VALORANT team NAVI Celestials strengthened their position with outstanding results in regional and international tournaments. NAVI also expanded their reach by re-forming a team in PUBG Mobile, as well as introducing a strong new roster in StarCraft 2, showing diversity and flexibility in the esports discipline. Actively involved in charity work, they continued to support Ukraine through social initiatives, raising funds to rebuild facilities damaged by the conflict. Overall, 2024 was a challenging and transformative year for NAVI, but they managed to maintain their reputation as one of the leading esports organizations in the world.
